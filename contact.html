About Us

In the late '90s and early 2000's a dedicated (OK, maybe obsessed may be a better description) group on individuals meet on an old board called Yahoo! Groups. We spent a few years, collecting, collating and comparing thousands of recording from old cassetes tapes and even reel-to-reel, always looking for a better quailty version of the episodes. The goal was to have a near perfect collection of audio files, with and without commericals, to enjoy and listen to over and over.

We collated data and compared notes with as many sourcesa as we could and created a database (spreadsheet really) of all the episodes, the reruns, who wrote which, who starred in an episode and a full description of the plot. In fact many of the websites that offer these episodes use our original data.

A few of us dusted off our collection and our data and built this website to sharew what we have at no cost. We do have a pateron site if you wish to help us "keep the lights on." For a $75 donation we'll even send you a thunmb drive with the entire collection for you to listen too any time, any where.

We hope you enjoy what we;ve built.
