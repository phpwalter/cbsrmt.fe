About

CBS Radio
Mystery Theater

CBS Radio Mystery Theater was a landmark radio anthology that brought the best in suspense, mystery, horror, crime, and the supernatural to life. Featuring original stories and adaptations from celebrated writers, the series captivated audiences with twists, atmosphere, and unforgettable characters.

Hosted by the distinguished E.G. Marshall, whose commanding voice became the signature of the series, each episode invited listeners into a world where the ordinary could turn extraordinary in an instant.

With rich storytelling, masterful performances, and theatrical sound design, CBS Radio Mystery Theater revived the golden age of radio drama for a new generation — and proved that a great story is timeless.

“Welcome to the sound of suspense.” — E.G. Marshall
